# In-village troop artwork

Original Clash of Clans in-game character renders, sourced from Clash Wiki on September 10–11, 2026. Downloaded PNGs were converted losslessly to WebP without changing the artwork. The images have native transparency. Troops are displayed inside the village, at building-relative scale.

Foot anchors are visually estimated from the original images and encoded in `dist/troop-assets.js`. These are single standing poses; patrol motion translates and mirrors the sprites, with subtle vertical bobbing. There is no claim of frame-by-frame walking animation.

Game artwork remains the property of Supercell and its respective owners. This is an unofficial event theme; the footer retains the Supercell affiliation notice and Fan Content Policy link. Event logos remain the organizers' supplied artwork.
